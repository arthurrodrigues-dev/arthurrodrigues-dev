## ☕ About Me

I'm a Computer Networks student at IFPB with a strong interest in IT infrastructure, network administration and cybersecurity.

My interest in technology goes beyond theory. I enjoy building virtualized environments, configuring services, troubleshooting network issues and understanding how systems communicate and operate.

Throughout my studies and hands-on projects, I've been developing practical knowledge in Linux administration, TCP/IP networking, routing and switching, network monitoring, virtualization and security assessment.

I'm particularly interested in understanding how enterprise infrastructure works, from network architecture and server administration to access control, monitoring and information security.

I believe that continuous learning, hands-on experience and technical curiosity are essential to becoming a better IT professional. I'm always looking for new challenges, practical labs and opportunities to expand my knowledge.
